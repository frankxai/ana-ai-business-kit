# Team working agreement workflow

Public-safe scaffold for owner review; not an authored Ana signature method or validated psychological intervention.

Before the session, select one observable problem: incomplete handoffs, decisions without owners, or unclear communication. Confirm facilitator, reporting boundary, and neutral alternative. Participants can pass without employment consequences. Do not record personal disclosures or individual psychological measurements. Active grievances, harassment allegations, and investigations belong in the organization's designated human process.

| Minutes | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Purpose, reporting limits, right to pass | Shared scope |
| 5–15 | Examine a fictional incomplete handoff | Observable friction |
| 15–30 | Map decisions and responsible roles | Decision map |
| 30–45 | Rehearse a clear request and readback | Communication practice |
| 45–55 | Propose two working agreements | Behaviors, owners, review dates |
| 55–60 | Confirm follow-through | 30-day plan |

Day 7: coordinator checks usability. Day 14: facilitator reviews process-level adoption. Day 30: team retains, amends, or retires agreements. Report only agreed process outputs and aggregate adoption counts. Suppress small-group feedback and free text where people could be identified; a numerical threshold alone does not guarantee anonymity. Individual reflection remains with the participant.

The [CIPD review](https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/evidence-reviews/trust-psychological-safety/) links trust and psychological safety with team behaviors and performance. This motivates exploring team practices; it does not prove this session causes those outcomes.

Use psychology through listening, perspective-taking, boundaries, and rehearsal. Neuroscience needs a precise source-supported educational claim and applicability review. Personal meaning and spiritual reflection belong to a separate opt-in offering. Psychotherapy requires its own practitioner scope, records, and care pathway.

`team-practice/v1` supports a draft only. Human declarations of voluntariness, no personal data, and completed content review are attestations, not automated verification. Agenda minutes must reconcile; each agreement needs a role owner and review date at or after the session. HTTPS evidence URLs require limitations. A source pointer is not permission to copy content.
