## Exercise

Read: “Can someone take this forward? The next team needs the pack soon. I think the main work is ready, but some inputs may still be missing.”

Ask: Which missing facts prevent the next role from acting? Which role decides, which role delivers, and which role receives? What must the receiving role confirm before accepting responsibility?

## Close and follow through

Ask the team to propose two observable working agreements. For each, record behavior, accountable role, and review date. Distinguish proposed commitments from accepted ones.

Proposed checkpoint owners: day 7, engagement coordinator; day 14, facilitator; day 30, practice owner with agreement owners. Confirm these roles in the private operating plan. Agreement-specific review dates are separate from the standard checkpoints and must be reconciled before delivery.

Possible process measure: complete handoffs divided by reviewed handoffs, with completeness defined before the trial. There is no supplied baseline or proven improvement. Avoid any reporting that exposes individual feedback or permits re-identification. Keep the source limitations from the session brief attached.
