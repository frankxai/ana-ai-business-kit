# Agency Productization

Package a workflow whose repeat delivery is evidenced. Preserve the human method, source provenance, support boundaries, and quality baseline. Sell implementation, adaptation, original permitted additions, and maintained service; do not claim exclusive rights to the public kit.

## Workflow

1. Identify one buyer, one recurring job, an accountable product owner, and at least two reviewed delivery receipts for the same workflow. Use synthetic receipts only for rehearsal. Without delivered evidence, return a hypothesis and a rehearsal plan; do not call it validated or ready for a commercial rollout.
2. Read [the product contract](references/product-contract.md). Define included artifacts, exclusions, adaptation owner, client/operator audience, delivery acceptance, update window, support window/hours, and stop conditions. Keep accommodation and event logistics as separately owned integrations.
3. Reuse `$team-practice-design` for voluntary team/AI practices, `$ana-research-library` for research provenance, and approved SOP outputs for operational products. Do not bundle employee data, clinical material, private reflection, or a live client template.
4. Specify attributable public-base material and separately owned additions. Public CC BY 4.0 materials remain reusable under their existing license. Identify third-party rights and remove identity-specific language for another agency. Ana's name, voice, image, authority, or endorsement is not licensed by this kit. An Ana co-launch is a proposal until her explicit scope, role, attribution and commercial agreement is recorded privately.
5. Adapt [the product brief](assets/product-brief.md) and [synthetic product record](assets/product.example.json). Use owner-supplied ceilings for operator hours, model/tool costs, support, and updates. Track contribution only from confirmed revenue and all delivery/support/tool/venue/payment/refund costs. Do not generate prices or imply a license creates passive income.
6. Validate from this skill directory:

```bash
python scripts/validate_product.py <private-product.json>
```

7. Route commercial facts to `$ana-hr-operations` SOP-04. Invoice drafts remain exclusively SOP-06. `$ana-template-studio` uses the validated and approved upstream stage to produce documents; `$ana-approved-content` drafts launch copy from separately approved evidence and rights. No publish or send follows from validation.

## Product choices

Use a practitioner workflow kit for agency delivery; a reviewed client session kit for participant use; a facilitator playbook for a host; or a bounded implementation service for a team adopting an AI workflow. A membership is justified by a recurring service and maintained update obligation, not by access to unchanged public files. Product tiers must state who operates the workflow and who takes responsibility for adaptation.

Reuse the existing operator/client package separation. A new product brief does not silently add files to either ZIP or make practitioner notes client-sendable. Link the existing [agency adaptation guide](../../../../docs/HR-AGENCY-ADAPTATION.md) when naming another operator.

## Completion receipt

Return `DRAFT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, buyer/job, delivery evidence and its limits, product boundaries, rights and attribution, resource ceilings, support/update end dates, adoption and quality metrics with denominators, blockers, and next reviewer/date. A valid record is a structured review draft, not proof of efficacy, approved pricing, installation, or a commercial license agreement.
