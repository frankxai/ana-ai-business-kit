# Product record contract

Use one product record per workflow/version. It is review preparation, not a contract generator or license grant. The validator rejects malformed records and explicit unsafe settings; natural-language rights, claims and private sources still require human review.

## Evidence and scope

Two distinct reviewed delivery receipts for the same workflow demonstrate repetition, not market demand or efficacy. Synthetic evidence supports a rehearsal only. Live receipts use private aliases; do not embed client facts or personal data. Each receipt records completed cases and quality-passed cases under the same rubric; disclose incomplete/failed cases rather than excluding them from the denominator.

Name buyer, job, product owner, adaptation owner, approval owner, acceptance rubric, exclusions, maximum implementation hours, model/tool spend ceiling, and maximum support hours. Support and updates each end at a stated date. Stop or rescope when a ceiling is reached; do not promise unlimited personal access.

## Rights and audience

The repository's existing [license](../../../../../LICENSE.md) covers reusable templates and documentation under CC BY 4.0. Preserve attribution, link the license, and identify changes. Never impose exclusive resale rights over that public base. Rights for code and separately added material must be reviewed explicitly; do not assume the documentation license grants rights over unrelated third-party code or assets.

Do not sublicense Ana's identity, credentials, voice, endorsement or client method without separate explicit rights. Another agency supplies its own identity, legal terms, data policy, source templates, named approver and local adaptations. Original paid additions need a rights reference; no reference means no claimed licensed additions.

Operator artifacts stay practitioner-only. Client materials require the existing client-package boundary and approval. A product can reference both audiences but must label each artifact individually. A clinician's services, treatment notes and participant spiritual reflections never enter this kit.

## Measurement

Use a declared eligible operator denominator for activation, raw case counts for first-pass quality, total delivery/rework/support hours per completed engagement, and actual model/tool spend. Set targets before the pilot; freeze cohort/window definitions. Compare quality to a reviewed pre-product baseline. Separate sales assumptions from paid receipts. Calculate contribution only after approved revenue and all relevant costs are available in finance systems through the existing SOPs.
