# Fewer meetings through better decisions

Replace recurring information exchange with a current work record and a response obligation. Protect conversations whose value is live judgment, relationship, negotiation, listening, or facilitated practice.

## Daily owner brief — proposed operating design

One brief contains: decisions due; blocked client commitments; material risks; ready deliverables; and changes since the previous snapshot. Every item has an owner, due date, exact evidence reference, and a requested action. Routine completed work remains available below the decision queue.

The coordinator prepares; Ana or the delegated accountable human decides. Batch routine approvals in a defined window without delaying urgent client or candidate commitments. The agency agrees the response window and escalation route; the planner does not fabricate an SLA or acceptance.

## Decision packet

Use the question, deadline, accountable owner, source versions, recommendation with rationale, alternatives with consequence, exact artifact/recipient where relevant, and the decision required. Record `pending`, `approved`, `changes_requested`, or `declined` with actual human evidence. Silence remains pending.

Separate “choose service scope,” “approve price,” “approve invoice,” and “choose a candidate.” A consolidated owner view does not merge their decision authorities. An employment offer follows the client's hiring process; an agency service proposal follows its commercial process.

## Meeting triage

| Meeting purpose | Default proposal | Replacement requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine status | Async candidate | Current source-linked update, owner, next action, response route |
| Bounded decision | Async decision candidate | Complete pre-read, explicit decision owner, deadline and response route |
| Interview | Keep live | Human recruiting process determines any format change |
| Relationship/client trust | Keep live | Organizer assesses relationship value |
| Facilitation/workshop | Keep live | Session design and participation choices remain intact |
| Conflict or care | Keep live | Appropriate human owner and private setting |
| Any explicitly live-required work | Keep live | Explicit organizer decision to change it |
| Missing/stale sources or unresolved dependencies | Repair pre-read | Obtain current evidence before proposing removal |

The local helper reports a proposal. It never cancels meetings, removes attendees, or sends follow-ups. A calendar event labeled “status” may contain an important conversation; the organizer reviews purpose before changing it.

For retained meetings: a pre-read before the meeting, only necessary attendees, a decision owner, and one reviewed output afterward. Use one approved capture system when recording is appropriate. Review note-sharing defaults and participant requirements before activation; do not duplicate sensitive transcripts across several note bots.

## Measure actual reduction

Baseline: two comparable working weeks, annotated by meeting purpose, participants, preparation, follow-up, and async work. Compare a two-week pilot at similar engagement/requisition volume. Track agency totals and Ana's own time separately.

- Calendar meeting minutes: duration × actual occurrence count; cancellations do not count as held.
- Team meeting cost in time: sum attendee minutes, with attendance/role scope stated.
- Net time recovered: reduction in meeting + preparation + follow-up minutes minus added async preparation, review, and rework minutes. Report workload differences and unknowns.
- Decision latency: median and p90 from a complete packet becoming ready to the actual human decision. Incomplete packet time is tracked separately, not hidden.
- Commitment reliability: accepted on-time deliverables / due deliverables, including overdue pending items.
- Owner dependency: escalations needing Ana / completed routine workflows; actual owner support minutes per week.

Proposed pilot targets: 30% fewer internal status-meeting minutes; 25% lower total coordination time per comparable engagement; 95% on-time client commitments; and no deterioration in decision latency or reviewed work quality. All baselines are unmeasured. These are experimental thresholds, not promised savings.

The planner's opportunity counts use scheduled duration and attendee count. They are not observed savings and omit replacement async work. Do not present those modeled counts as the pilot result. Stop a meeting reduction experiment when decision latency, service quality, or unresolved workload worsens.
