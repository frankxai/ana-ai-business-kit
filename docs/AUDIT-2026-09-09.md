# HR plugin audit and research — 9 September 2026

Scope: source inspection, local tests, and primary-source market/architecture research. This is not a production, customer-data, accounting, or clinical audit.

## Verified starting point

Canonical source: `frankxai/ana-ai-business-kit`, base commit `5ee80906a8079a797eb7c737401f5c9a74f4185e` (16 July 2026). The main branch contains an installable Codex plugin, four skills, eight HR SOPs, research/content/template validators, and two optional facilitation packages. Main had no later commits at inspection. Existing draft PR #8 concerns the editorial contract and remains separate.

The existing baseline `npm test` passed. That did not expose the numerical and malformed-input defects below. Tests are evidence for covered behavior, not a general production guarantee.

## Demonstrated defects addressed in this candidate

| Finding | Consequence | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nonfinite/negative amounts and wrong-shaped records were inadequately handled | Invalid arithmetic or tracebacks | Bounded finite decimals, typed failures, CLI regressions |
| Quantities and rates were rounded to cents before multiplication | Incorrect fractional billing | Preserve quantity/rate precision; round money explicitly |
| Rounding depended on decimal defaults | Host-dependent results | Isolated half-up arithmetic context |
| Numeric strings passed validation but failed formatting | Draft renderer failure | Shared decimal parsing/formatting |
| Invoice context selected offer payment terms | Wrong terms on invoice draft | Document-specific invoice context |

Candidate `0.3.0-rc.1` also adds `ana-team-practice`: a fifth skill, strict team-level brief, fictional rehearsal, draft renderer, and follow-through dates. It adds no network calls or employer record collection.

## Gaps recorded at initial audit

Invoice-number uniqueness, approved billable-event linkage, deposits/partial billing, mixed-tax lines, credit notes, paid-state reconciliation, duplicate retries, authenticated approvals, tenant isolation, and an accounting connector are not implemented. An approver name in JSON is not proof of identity or approval of the current artifact revision. Optional template state checks do not replace the live template visual review.

Research and privacy validators check structure, known keys, and declarations. They do not prove source truth or detect every sensitive fact hidden in free text. The new team workflow has the same limitation and requires human content review.

Package versions are distinct: legacy download manifest `1.1.0`, former plugin `0.2.0+codex.20260714123709`, new plugin candidate `0.3.0-rc.1`. Do not describe the template documentation label “v1.2” as a verified end-to-end product release version.

## Research findings and resulting decisions

All sources below were inspected on 9 September 2026. Vendor material describes product positioning, not independent evidence of effectiveness.

| Source | What it supports | Product decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Anthropic HR plugin](https://claude.com/plugins/human-resources) | Generic recruiting, onboarding, review, compensation, and policy workflows already exist | Differentiate through agency delivery, billing evidence, Ana-approved practice, and implementation quality |
| [Culture Amp development](https://www.cultureamp.com/platform/perform/employee-development-plans) | Role clarity and manager development are established product categories | Sell concrete role/communication practices and use the customer's systems of record |
| [NeuroLeadership Institute](https://www.neuroleadership.com/articles/latest-from-the-lab-creating-psychological-safety-for-improved-performance) | Provider translates research into behavioral leadership offerings | Benchmark method-to-practice packaging; do not reproduce proprietary frameworks or borrow credentials |
| [CIPD evidence review](https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/evidence-reviews/trust-psychological-safety/) | Trust and psychological safety relate to team behavior and performance | Track observable agreements and adoption; do not promise causal effects from our unvalidated session |
| [MCP authorization](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2026-07-28/tutorials/security/authorization) and [security considerations](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/basic/authorization/security-considerations) | Protected resources use scoped authorization, token audience checks, and no token passthrough | MCP becomes a narrow adapter over a permissioned domain service when justified |
| [European Commission AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) | Employment uses require careful classification; workplace emotion recognition is among prohibited practices, subject to statutory scope/exceptions | Exclude emotion inference and automated employment decisions; obtain use-case review before expanding scope |
| [EDPB design/default guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-42019-on-article-25-data-protection-by-design-and-by-default_en) | Data minimization and access controls belong in design | Separate corporate operations, personal reflection, and any professional care records |
| [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Existing licensed templates may be commercially adapted with attribution | Charge for additional value; retain existing public rights and identity limits |
| [Lemon Squeezy policy](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products) | Digital goods are supported; services, certain resale products, and marketplace arrangements are prohibited | Keep consulting/workshops on a suitable direct service billing route; review exact digital SKU and seller eligibility |

No competitor paid accounts, performance benchmarks, or customer demand studies were performed. These findings inform positioning and architecture; pricing and adoption targets are proposed experiments.

## Continuation — local billing review candidate

Candidate `0.3.0-rc.2` adds a local SQLite preparation ledger: exact source-file digest matching, immutable event IDs, full-event amount reconciliation, invoice-number reservations within that ledger, artifact/engagement/revision-bound manual acknowledgments, and transaction-safe preparation retries. The source and reviewer references remain unverified human declarations. This closes a bounded local preparation gap; authenticated authorization, cumulative budgets, cross-ledger/provider duplicate controls, tenant isolation, accounting integration, and settlement remain open.
