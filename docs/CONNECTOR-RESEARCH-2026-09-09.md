# Ana agency connector decision — verified 9 September 2026

**Decision:** preserve Ana's existing ATS and document suite. Buy their native identity, record storage, calendar, transcription and accounting capabilities. Build the agency-specific decision queue, evidence contracts, approved actions, exception routing and outcome measurement around them. MCP availability alone does not justify an ATS migration. No connection, authentication, live-data access or integration test was performed in this research; the capabilities below are documented vendor capabilities.

| Layer | Verified capability and material limit | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Recruit CRM | Remote MCP at `https://agent.recruitcrm.io/mcp`; Business/Enterprise plans. Per-user OAuth and existing role permissions; role needs MCP Access. Supports search and create/update for candidates, jobs, companies, contacts, tasks, meetings, notes and call logs, plus hotlists and pipeline context. No delete actions. **MCP-originated writes are not currently visible in Recruit CRM's audit log.** | Strong agency fit because the product combines ATS and client CRM. Keep initial pilot read/draft-only; require our receipt log before enabling specific writes. [Product](https://recruitcrm.io/recruit-crm-mcp/), [operation and audit limits](https://help.recruitcrm.io/en/articles/14485570-recruit-crm-mcp-model-context-protocol). |
| Ashby | Official MCP launched June 29, 2026 in Open Beta on all plans. User-level OAuth, acting as the individual user with existing permissions. Reads candidates, applications, jobs, interviews, offers, notes, feedback, emails and hiring teams; selected writes include notes, interview-stage changes and considering candidates for jobs. | Better alternative when Ana works inside a client's established in-house hiring stack. Use native Ashby workflows when work stays in Ashby. Beta status, exact tool catalogue, OAuth scope strings and write-audit detail require tenant verification; the linked setup guide was not text-readable in this audit. [Release](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/mcp), [setup](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/ashby-mcp-server-beta). |
| Notion | Hosted OAuth MCP at `https://mcp.notion.com/mcp` reads/writes under the user's full existing permissions. Interactive authorization remains required. Enterprise provides approved-client governance; a filtered view is not a narrower authorization boundary. | Optional operating handbook and decision board; retain authoritative documents in Drive/SharePoint. Do not connect Ana's unrestricted personal identity to a general employer knowledge agent. [Connection](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/get-started-with-mcp), [permissions](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/notion-mcp). |

Notion's current MCP goes beyond Notion-only search: `notion-ai-search` can query connected, accessible sources including Drive, Mail, Calendar and Slack. It requires Notion AI; connecting third-party apps to Notion AI requires Business/Enterprise. `notion-fetch` with `self` reports actual tool entitlement. Connected-app search results cannot be fetched through `notion-fetch`, so use the original source connector to verify crucial evidence. `notion-query-meeting-notes` requires Business or higher with Notion AI. Advertised Custom Agent session tools require separate capability, AI and agent access. Availability in a tool list does not prove entitlement. [Tool contracts](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/mcp-supported-tools), [connector plans](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/notion-ai-connectors).

**Meeting reduction:** replace routine updates with source-linked briefs, an owner, due date and explicit decision request. Transcription reduces follow-up effort only for meetings still worth holding; it does not remove meeting demand by itself.

For a Google company, use Calendar booking and Meet notes before adding another meeting bot. Meet produces notes in the organizer's Drive and attaches them to the calendar event; access depends on document sharing. English and Spanish are supported, but only one spoken language at a time. The organizer needs an eligible plan and admin enablement. Current business eligibility includes Business Standard/Plus and Enterprise Standard/Plus. Calendar permits one booking page on Business Starter; Standard/Plus adds multiple schedules, reminders and checking several calendars. These are native capabilities, not a verified Google MCP integration. [Meet behavior](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931), [plan matrix](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129), [booking limits](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/16287038).

Notion Meeting Notes is an alternative when the team already works in Notion: Business/Enterprise, notes private by default, optional internal-participant auto-sharing, desktop captures system audio plus microphone while browser captures microphone only. Use one capture system per meeting and preserve the access boundary of the original conversation. [Meeting notes](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes).

For an existing Microsoft company, retain Teams/SharePoint/Outlook. Teams intelligent recap needs Teams plus Teams Premium or Microsoft Copilot licensing and enabled transcription; full recap also needs recording policy. Do not buy a second office suite for this feature. [Microsoft prerequisites](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/intelligent-recap-calls-meetings).

**Workflow engine:** choose one only after the first manual workflow exposes the missing execution requirement.

- **n8n** suits operator-maintained cross-app workflows. Official MCP supports workflow discovery, execution and editing; OAuth or personal API-token authentication. Workflows must be individually exposed. User permissions apply, but a given user's clients cannot receive different per-workflow exposure; search can return previews of other workflows that user can view. Keep builder/edit credentials out of Ana's daily runtime. The official docs identify version-dependent controls and agent features still in Preview. Confirm installed version, granted permissions and project isolation in a fixture tenant. Native official skills exist. [MCP controls](https://docs.n8n.io/connect/connect-to-n8n-mcp-server).
- **Trigger.dev** suits a coded service requiring retryable tasks, queues and waits. Its MCP is a developer control plane for deploy/run/inspect, authenticated through CLI, with project, dev-only and read-only restrictions. Its waitpoint token can resume an approval workflow; our authenticated approval service must still bind the approver and document digest. Idempotency keys expire (default 30 days), and failed runs clear their key: an accounting/event ledger must enforce lasting duplicate prevention independently. [MCP](https://trigger.dev/docs/mcp-introduction), [waitpoints](https://trigger.dev/docs/wait-for-token), [idempotency](https://trigger.dev/docs/idempotency).

**Build boundary:** role-scoped retrieval adapters; structured role/client/offer/decision records; approved-source provenance; exact-revision action receipts; exception queues; metric events. Buy ATS CRUD, conferencing, identity and payment infrastructure. Candidate lists remain in restricted hiring records; general company knowledge receives only approved process material and necessary aggregate workflow facts. Personal spiritual work and clinical material remain separate. Rehearse permissions, revoked access, stale sources, duplicate requests and blocked writes before using live company data.
